### Proven path-by-path mechanism
1. **Choose/change app menu:** `SCPCarSplit+Panel.m:156–168 paneChoose:` → `Picker.m:247–328 showPickerForSlot:` (even if `hasContent=1`) → `Picker.m:403–432 refreshPickerSheet` → `Picker.m:443ff buildPickerSheetForPane:`. Picker is a full **pane-local** `UIView` lavender `alpha=1.0`, appended above `pane.host`, with a plus label. Unlike a scene teardown, this route does not call `detachVC`, `backgroundScene` or native VC.hidden. The app can continue audio and input while its pixels are covered. `Picker.m:330–338 removePickerFromPane:` nils its property immediately, fades previous view **0.15s**, removes on animation completion; `pickerCancel:` restores occupied pane without closing it. Picker sheet is an independent 542x242-ish popover at `UIWindowLevelStatusBar+65` (~1065), alpha1, blur/mesh face. Its effect can obscure content in its footprint even if pane cover is absent. `removePickerSheet` fades it ~0.22s, checks for another open popup before hiding the tray window. SOURCE+original ARM64 disassembly confirm these mechanisms existed before HL5; they are **not later-added layers**.
2. **Dragging divider (change ratios):** `SCPCarSplit+Divider.m:122–127 dividerPan` → `beginResize:56–61` → `Picker.m:198–233 addCoverToPane:` only if pane.vc exists. It creates lavender full-pane `pane.cover` with tag-77 app icon, `insertSubview:aboveSubview:pane.host`, becomes alpha1 in 0.12s. `SCPCarSplit.m:508–517 markDismissSlot:` only recolors this *same* cover/tints icon; no additional full-pane layer. During `Changed`, `Divider.m:195–201` calls `relayoutAnimated:NO pushScenes:NO`, and `Relayout.m:131–134` therefore skips native `pushSceneSizes` while still sending hosted `pushHostFramesLive:YES`. `endResize:63–77` at release schedules `removeCoverFromPane` after **0.45s** if resize remains stopped; `removeCoverFromPane:235–243` then fades cover over **0.22s**. Repeated resize may defer cleanup while `self.resizing=YES`; exact on-car retention still unmeasured. Independent original `beginPaneDrag` adds *small* `dragGhost` plus reuses `beginResize`, not a second large cover.
